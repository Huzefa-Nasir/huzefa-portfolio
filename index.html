Selected work

Real automation problems. Serious workflow logic.

I don't want clients to guess whether I can handle complexity. These examples show the kinds of systems I build.

Advanced n8n · AI Lead Generation

End-to-end LinkedIn prospecting, lead scoring and personalized outreach

A self-hosted n8n workflow using the AnySite LinkedIn community node to find prospects matching an ICP, enrich and analyze lead data, score potential clients with AI, prioritize them and prepare personalized outreach. It combines branching logic, AI processing, Google Sheets and multiple automation stages.

ICP→LinkedIn discovery→Enrichment→AI scoring→Prioritization→Personalized outreach
Zapier · AI Voice

Automated outbound calling pipeline

Lead records are pulled from Google Sheets, formatted, authenticated against a third-party AI voice service and looped through for automated outbound calls.

Sheets→Formatter→Auth→Loop→AI Voice
Email Parser · Azure AI · Postmark

Unstructured email turned into structured operations

Forwarded emails are captured with Zapier Email Parser, processed with code and Azure AI, filtered into the fields the client needs, then rebuilt as structured messages and sent to a third party through Postmark.

Email→Parser→Azure AI→Filter→Postmark
HubSpot · API · Python

Create-or-update CRM synchronization

An incoming webhook is processed with AI and Python, matched against HubSpot and routed into separate create-contact or update-contact paths.

Webhook→AI→Python→Search HubSpot→Create / Update
YouTube API · Local Automation

Folder-to-YouTube publishing automation

A local API-driven system uploads videos automatically, uses AI to select titles and descriptions, and schedules publishing. The operator only supplies the source folder location through Excel.

Excel→Folder→AI metadata→YouTube API→Schedule
Large Zap · Law Firm Routing

Complex multi-path lead distribution by case type, client status and sales intent

A long Zapier workflow filters incoming law-firm leads through multiple layers of logic — case type, existing-client status, sales-pitch detection and other conditions — before routing each lead to the appropriate attorney or destination.

Lead→Qualification→Case type→Existing client?→Sales pitch?→Attorney routing
