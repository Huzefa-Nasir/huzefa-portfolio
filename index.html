Advanced n8n · AI Lead Generation

End-to-end LinkedIn prospecting, lead scoring and personalized outreach

A self-hosted n8n workflow using a LinkedIn community node to find prospects matching an ICP, enrich and analyze lead data, score potential clients with AI, prioritize them and prepare personalized outreach. It combines branching logic, AI processing, Google Sheets and multiple automation stages.

ICP→LinkedIn discovery→Enrichment→AI scoring→Prioritization→Personalized outreach
Zapier · AI Voice

Automated outbound calling pipeline

Lead records are pulled from Google Sheets, formatted, authenticated against a third-party AI voice service and looped through for automated outbound calls.

Sheets→Formatter→Auth→Loop→AI Voice
Email Parser · Azure AI · Postmark

Unstructured email turned into structured operations

Forwarded emails are captured with Zapier Email Parser, processed with code and Azure AI, filtered into the fields the client needs, then rebuilt as structured messages and sent to a third party through Postmark.

Email→Parser→Azure AI→Filter→Postmark
HubSpot · API · Python

Create-or-update CRM synchronization

An incoming webhook is processed with AI and Python, matched against HubSpot and routed into separate create-contact or update-contact paths.

Webhook→AI→Python→HubSpot search→Create / Update
YouTube API · AI · Local Automation

Folder-to-YouTube automated publishing

A local workflow uses the YouTube API to upload videos, generate/select titles and descriptions with AI, and schedule publishing based on a folder location supplied through Excel.

Excel→Folder→AI metadata→YouTube API→Schedule
Advanced Zapier · Law Firm Routing

Large multi-path lead routing system for attorney assignment

A complex Zap evaluates leads using case type, existing-client status, sales intent and other conditions, then sends each lead through the correct path to the appropriate attorney or destination. This is the kind of workflow where logic design matters as much as connecting apps.

Lead→Qualify→Existing client?→Case type→Path logic→Attorney
